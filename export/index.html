How export enquiries work

Share the country you serve, the pack sizes and approximate quantities you need, and your timeline. We will reply with the next steps. Destination markets, documentation and regulatory requirements are confirmed individually for each enquiry.

Why source Malabar pepper from India

India's Malabar coast is one of the world's historic pepper-growing regions, with centuries of trade. Sourcing from Kerala means pepper from the region where it has long been grown and traded.
