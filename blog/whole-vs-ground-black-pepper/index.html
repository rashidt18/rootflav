Whole vs Ground Black Pepper: Which Should You Buy?

Black pepper is sold both as whole peppercorns and as ready-ground powder. Both have their place, but they behave differently in the kitchen.

Aroma and flavour

The aroma and heat of pepper are held inside the peppercorn. Once the pepper is ground, those aromatic compounds are exposed to air and begin to fade. Whole peppercorns that are crushed just before cooking give a stronger, fresher flavour.

Shelf life

Whole peppercorns stay fragrant for much longer than ground pepper when stored well. Ground pepper is more convenient but loses its aroma faster, so it is best bought in smaller quantities and used sooner.

Convenience

Ground pepper is quick to measure and mixes evenly into rubs, batters and spice blends. Whole peppercorns need a mill or mortar, but you can grind exactly the amount you need.

Quick guide

UseBetter choice
Table seasoningWhole, freshly ground
Rasam, stocks, biryani, pulaoWhole or lightly crushed
Marinades and rubsCoarsely crushed or ground
Bakery and quick mixesGround
Long-term storageWhole

Our suggestion

Keep whole peppercorns as your main supply and grind small amounts as needed. Store them in an airtight container away from heat, moisture and sunlight. You can find our options on the Malabar black pepper page.
