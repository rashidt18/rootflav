Everyday Cooking with Black Pepper

Black pepper is one of the most used spices in the world, and in Kerala it has been part of the kitchen for centuries. A few simple habits make a big difference to how it tastes.

Crush it fresh

Whole peppercorns hold their aroma inside until they are crushed. Crack or grind them just before use with a mortar and pestle, a pepper mill or the flat of a heavy knife, and the flavour is noticeably stronger than ready-ground pepper.

Toast lightly for depth

Warm whole peppercorns in a dry pan for a minute or two until fragrant, then cool and crush. This deepens the flavour and works well in masalas, spice blends and rubs.

When to add it

  • At the start of cooking: whole peppercorns in tempering, stocks, rasam, pulao and biryani release flavour slowly into the dish.
  • Near the end: freshly ground pepper keeps its sharp aroma when added to eggs, soups, curries and salads just before serving.

Dishes that use black pepper

Pepper features in rasam, pepper chicken and pepper fry, biryani and pulao, egg preparations, soups, pickles, marinades and masala chai. It also works in simple dishes, such as on buttered toast, in yoghurt dips or over fresh fruit.
